Is Now a Good Time to Buy a House in the Antelope Valley?

A clear summary of what this guide covers and who it is for.

By Nick Armijo August 14, 2026

How Much Does It Cost to Buy a House in Palmdale, CA?

f you're thinking about buying a home in Palmdale, one of your first questions is probably: How much does it actually cost to buy a house here? As of June 2026, the typical Palmdale home value is approximately $505,741, while the median listing price is around $524,648. But the price of the home is only one part of what you'll need to consider. Your neighborhood, down payment, loan type, closing costs, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and mortgage rate can all affect what buying a home in Palmdale actually costs.

What Is the Average Home Price in Palmdale, California?

The typical home value in Palmdale, California is approximately $505,741 as of June 2026, according to Zillow's Home Value Index. Palmdale home values are down about 0.2% compared with the previous year. The median listing price was approximately $524,648, while Zillow reported a median sale price of approximately $497,333 for May 2026. These numbers are useful for understanding the overall Palmdale housing market, but they don't mean every buyer needs a $500,000 budget. Home prices vary significantly depending on location, property size, condition, lot size, upgrades, and other features.

What Can $400,000 Buy in Palmdale?

A budget around $400,000 can still provide home-buying options in Palmdale, although buyers should expect the available properties to vary in size, condition, location, and features. Recent Palmdale sales show homes selling in the low-$400,000 range, including three-bedroom properties in ZIP codes such as 93550 and 93552. At this price point, flexibility can make a major difference. A buyer who is open to different parts of Palmdale, smaller properties, or homes that need some updating may have more options than someone searching for a specific neighborhood or property type. Instead of asking only, “Can I buy a house for $400,000?” I recommend asking, “What does my $400,000 budget currently buy in Palmdale?” That's a much more useful question because the answer changes with available inventory.

What Can $500,000 Buy in Palmdale?

A budget around $500,000 puts a buyer close to Palmdale's current typical home value, which can open the door to a wider selection of properties. Recent sales around this range have included three- and four-bedroom homes across different parts of Palmdale. However, two homes with the same $500,000 price can offer very different square footage, lot sizes, upgrades, and locations. Palmdale also has meaningful price differences between areas. For example, Zillow's June 2026 data shows a typical home value of approximately $595,828 in ZIP code 93551, compared with approximately $455,895 in 93550 and $499,560 in 93552. That's why I recommend deciding what matters most to you before setting a search area: price, space, neighborhood, commute, home condition, or specific property features.

What Can $600,000 Buy in Palmdale?

A $600,000 home budget in Palmdale can provide access to properties above the city's current typical home value and may give buyers more choices when it comes to size, location, condition, and amenities. Recent sales in and around the upper-$500,000 and $600,000 range include larger three- and four-bedroom properties, particularly in parts of West Palmdale and ZIP code 93551. That doesn't mean every $600,000 property will automatically be larger or better for your needs. Location and property characteristics still have a major influence on price. The advantage of a higher budget is usually having more choices and the ability to compare which features are actually worth paying more for.

How Much Down Payment Do I Need to Buy a House in Palmdale?

You do not necessarily need a 20% down payment to buy a house in Palmdale. The amount you'll need depends on the mortgage program you qualify for, your finances, lender requirements, and the property you're purchasing. For perspective, on a hypothetical $500,000 home:

  • A 3% down payment would equal $15,000.
  • A 3.5% down payment would equal $17,500.
  • A 5% down payment would equal $25,000.
  • A 10% down payment would equal $50,000.
  • A 20% down payment would equal $100,000.

Those examples are only down-payment calculations. They don't represent a loan quote or the total amount of cash you'll need to close. Buyers should speak with a qualified lender to determine which mortgage programs they actually qualify for and what their estimated cash-to-close would be.

What Other Costs Should I Expect When Buying a Home?

The purchase price and down payment aren't the only costs involved in buying a home in Palmdale. Depending on your transaction and financing, your total cost may also include expenses such as closing costs, lender fees, appraisal fees, inspections, prepaid property taxes, homeowners insurance, and other transaction-related expenses. There are also ongoing costs after purchasing the property. Your monthly housing expense may include your mortgage principal and interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance when applicable, HOA dues when applicable, utilities, and maintenance. That's why two buyers purchasing homes for the exact same price can end up with different upfront and monthly costs.

How Much Income Do I Need to Buy a $500,000 House in Palmdale?

There isn't one income that automatically qualifies someone to buy a $500,000 home in Palmdale. Mortgage qualification depends on factors including your income, existing debts, credit profile, down payment, interest rate, loan program, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and other monthly obligations. Instead of choosing a home price based solely on annual salary, I recommend starting with the monthly housing payment you're comfortable carrying. A lender can then help determine what purchase-price range may fit your finances. Once you know that number, a local home search becomes much more useful.

Is It Cheaper to Buy a House in East or West Palmdale?

Home prices can vary considerably between different parts of Palmdale, so buyers shouldn't assume the entire city has one price range. For example, Zillow's June 2026 data shows typical values around $595,828 in 93551, which includes much of West Palmdale, compared with approximately $455,895 in 93550 and $499,560 in 93552. Those figures are broad ZIP-code averages rather than prices for individual neighborhoods or homes. The better approach is to compare properties that fit your actual requirements across multiple areas. Sometimes expanding a search by only a few miles can change the amount of space or features available within the same budget.
